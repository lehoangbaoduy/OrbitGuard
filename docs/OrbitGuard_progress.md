**Headline per research claim** (test split; learned models 3 training seeds × 10 scenario seeds = 30 episodes per cell, baselines 10; paired tests on the 10 matched scenario seeds, training seeds averaged per scenario; tables in `experiments/results/`, corrected rendering in `experiments/results_posthoc/`).

1. **Adaptive vs fixed margin (PRD §2, the central claim): inconclusive; no improvement is shown.**

   - Collision-or-unsafe rate, adaptive vs fixed: normal 0.000 vs 0.000; noise_increase **0.267 vs 0.467** (difference −0.200, 95 % CI [−0.456, 0.056], Wilcoxon p = 0.094); density 0.367 vs 0.400 (p = 1.0); simultaneous 0.100 vs 0.100 (p = 1.0); capability 0.433 vs 0.400 (p = 1.0).

2. **Shield (secondary contribution, separately trained no-shield ablation, P6-M1): supported for the collision-or-unsafe rate (unadjusted paired tests, post hoc; none survives the table-wide Holm adjustment), at a Δv cost.**

Per condition the collision difference is not significant (p ≥ 0.125), so no collision-rate effect is claimed; the pooled count is descriptive only (episodes cluster by scenario and training seed).

3. **Against the non-learned baselines:** adaptive MAPPO has a lower collision-or-unsafe rate than No-Maneuver in all five conditions (p 0.0039–0.031) and than Rule-Based in four (not under noise_increase, p = 0.73), always at higher Δv (+0.14 to +5.39 m/s). Rule-Based never collides. No-Maneuver collides in 13 of 50 episodes.
4. **Graceful degradation under shift (PRD §2 contribution 2, §34): not demonstrated.** Both margin arms have a zero normal-condition rate, so the §34 degradation ratio is undefined (n/a) for every collision and collision-or-unsafe cell of both; the corrected `degradation.md` shows the absolute change instead. Each condition draws its own 10 test seeds, so normal-vs-shift contrasts are unpaired.
5. **The adaptive margin barely adapts outside the noise shift.** Decision-time d_safe in the step logs (162 000 per-satellite values per cell, recomputed): at its floor 1070.7 m in 99.5 % of normal steps, 99.0 % simultaneous, 99.1 % capability, 50.6 % density (mean 1107 m, p95 1171 m); under noise the floor moves to 1212.1 m (98.4 % of steps at it). Fixed-Margin is 1071.0 m everywhere. The congestion (ρ) and risk (R) terms are therefore almost unexercised, and the noise result compares a ~1212 m margin with a 1071 m one; no Fixed-Margin control at ~1212 m exists (Q57).

**Caveats that must travel with any of these numbers.**
- **The grid selected on Δv budget alone.** All 8 R5 points had 0 collision and 0 unsafe episodes on 20 validation episodes, so the extended score reduced to 0.25 × budget fraction (winner 0.0289, range 0.0289–0.0850; one training seed, 25 iterations).
- **Asymmetric tuning.** The adaptive coefficients were tuned; the Fixed-Margin constant was matched afterwards to the tuned adaptive mean (Q30/Q47). No constant sweep was run for the fixed arm.
- **One yardstick for "unsafe".** Every model is scored against the *default-config* adaptive margin (Q33, Q52): about 1141 m at nominal noise. A model whose own margin is 1071 m is held to a stricter standard than its own controller uses; a model at 1212 m (adaptive under noise) to a looser one. Sensitivity under each model's own margin needs re-scoring with new runs (Q60).
- **"Unsafe rate" is collision-or-unsafe**, decided at each pair's closest approach (`experiment.py` `_derived`, `episode_metrics.py`). The corrected tables say so.
- **Mission deviation is belief-based** and noise-sensitive: No-Maneuver reads 0.140 (normal) and 0.448 (noise) although it never acts. It is not a mission-performance measure under the noise shift (Q59).
- **Low power.** Three training seeds (t with 2 df) and 10 scenario seeds. With 80 paired tests in the table and no correction pre-registered, a post-hoc Holm adjustment over the whole table leaves **no** p below 0.05 (smallest attainable raw p is 0.00195 > 0.05/80); among the five adaptive-vs-fixed collision-or-unsafe tests the smallest raw p is 0.094 (with the five Δv tests added, it is Δv under noise, p = 0.037, which costs Δv rather than saving it).

- **Validation subsets.** The R5 grid used the first 20 and the R6 validation pass the first 10 of the 50 validation seeds (prereg R5, `run_experiments.sh --max-seeds 10`), not the whole §33 pool (§9, 2026-11-04).
- **Shift magnitudes and several rules are PROVISIONAL-Astro** (Q29, Q36, Q44–Q47); N = 15 only.
- **Test exposure.** The disclosure of 2026-11-03 (§9: scenario composition read during the wave-4 review, before the freeze) stands, and the post-freeze audit replayed five test seeds (§9, 2026-11-04).

**Anomalies, and whether they are explained.**
- No-Maneuver collisions 0.100 (normal) → 0.000 (noise): **explained**, different seeds per condition (verified disjoint), not a noise effect.
- No-Maneuver mission deviation 0.140 → 0.448 under noise: **explained**, belief-based shadow (above).
- Shielded models: collision-or-unsafe 0 in normal, 0.10–0.47 under shifts: **partly explained.** Most shielded episodes have at least one step where no feasible repair existed (adaptive: 70 % normal, 67 % noise, 90 % density, 97 % simultaneous, 77 % capability; Q23 fallback) and the budget runs out in 16/30 density and 25/30 capability episodes; under noise the shield also acts on beliefs whose error grows with the noise. Not apportioned between causes.
- Adaptive spends more Δv under noise than fixed in each training seed (fleet means 2.65 / 2.58 / 2.92 vs 2.12 / 2.08 / 1.31 m/s): consistent with its larger margin, but the policy also sees d_safe as an input outside its training range, so the mechanism is not isolated.
- `existing_conjunction_worsened` is labelled on 82 (adaptive), 80 (fixed) and 42 (no-shield) of 150 episodes; a link to Q23 fallback approvals is plausible but rests on one replayed episode.

18. **R_safety (and the coordination term's "post" value): computed from a conjunction assessment taken before or after the agents' actions are applied?** Resolved via a causally-ordered pipeline: the assessment computed at the *start* of a control step (before actions) is that step's "pre" value, feeding both that step's observations and (saved for next step) the reward's coordination baseline; a *second* assessment computed immediately after the physical control step (post-maneuver, post-propagation) is both that step's reward "post" value (feeding `R_safety` and the coordination term) AND the *next* step's "pre" — i.e., consecutive steps' assessments chain directly, with reward computed as a function of the (pre, action, post) transition, matching standard RL semantics. *Update 2026-11-02 (Q34, `acbc3c1`):* R_safety and the coordination term's "post" are no longer the realized post-step assessment. In both shield arms they are scored on the predicted outcome of the raw joint proposal from the decision-time beliefs (`conjunction/pair_assessment.assess_proposal`). The realized post-step assessment still becomes the next step's "pre", the next observation and the outcome metrics.
19. **Coordination term's global "pre"/"post" risk (Q13's `total_risk_pre`/`total_risk_post`): summed over which assessment set?** Resolved: summed over all `PairAssessment.risk_proxy` values across the *entire* candidate-pair set (not per-agent), from the same pre/post assessment pair described in Q18 — a single shared scalar term applied identically to every agent's reward that step, matching PRD §25's description of coordination as a *global* term. *Update 2026-11-02:* the "post" sum now comes from the predicted outcome of the raw proposal (see the Q34 update under Q18).
20. **Capability enforcement (checklist #15, "NO_MANEUVER never an automatic fallback"): how do `maneuver_enabled=False` and an insufficient `remaining_delta_v_mps` budget interact with the chosen action?** Resolved in `rl/environment.py::_apply_actions`: `maneuver_enabled=False` zeroes the applied delta-v entirely regardless of the chosen intent (the satellite physically cannot maneuver); an enabled-but-insufficient budget **caps the applied magnitude** at `remaining_delta_v_mps` (direction preserved from the proposed delta-v) rather than silently substituting `NO_MANEUVER` — a partial burn in the intended direction is always closer to the agent's actual intent than doing nothing. `max_delta_v_per_action_mps` is passed directly as `map_intent_to_delta_v`'s `base_delta_v_mps` parameter, naturally tying per-action magnitude to the satellite's own capability.

21. **Delta-v reward term: charged on the requested or the budget-capped burn?** Resolved (found during P3-M5 tuning): on the REQUESTED burn (`environment._compute_rewards`). With an applied-only charge, once the budget is spent every action is a physical no-op, the agent pays nothing for asking, and ~93% of steps carried no learning signal.
22. **Shield repair: do uninvolved agents keep their proposals or get reset to NO_MANEUVER (PRD §29 step 6 says "leave uninvolved agents at NO_MANEUVER")?** Resolved: they KEEP their proposed action; only the *affected* agents (in a violating pair, or with a capability/mission-limit violation) are searched. Literal reset can silently re-create the very conjunction an uninvolved pair's burn was already resolving. A deliberate, documented departure from the PRD's wording (`safety/shield.py` docstring).
23. **Shield repair when NO feasible joint alternative exists (the PRD is silent, and forbids NO_MANEUVER as a default fallback)?** Resolved: approve the capability-valid assignment ranked best by (feasible, risk, margin shortfall, delta-v, lowest moving index) and report `feasible=False` (logged as `shield_infeasible_steps`). Found by test: with tiny sensing noise the risk proxy saturates and a risk-only fallback degenerated to NO_MANEUVER; the margin-shortfall term (summed depth inside `d_safe`, 0 for feasible sets) fixes it. Evidence: zero-noise conjunction seeds that collide 5/7 unshielded collide 0/7 shielded.
24. **Repair-search ordering and tolerances.** Risk compared after quantizing to 1e-3 so negligible differences cost no fuel; ties then fall to shortfall (1 m), total delta-v (1e-9), then the lexicographically lowest tuple of maneuvering satellite indices, then the assignment tuple (a total order, so nothing depends on iteration order). Beam expansion order: highest-risk violating conjunction first (PRD §26 priority), index as tie-break.
